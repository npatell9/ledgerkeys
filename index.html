What it does

Free

Go anywhere by typing

Invoices, bills, bank accounts, contacts, chart of accounts, journals, reports. A few letters and Enter.

Pro

Your own shortcuts

Save any Xero page you open often, such as a client's VAT return or an aged payables report, under a name you choose.

Pro

A colour for each client

Give each organisation a colour. A band at the top of the screen shows whose books you are in, so nothing gets posted to the wrong client.

Pro

Recent pages

Jump back to the last ten pages you worked on.

Your client data stays in Xero

LedgerKeys asks Chrome for access to xero.com and nothing else. Your shortcuts and colours are stored in your own browser. It sends nothing from your Xero pages to us or to anyone else.
