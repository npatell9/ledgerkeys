LedgerKeys Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

LedgerKeys is a Chrome extension that adds a command palette, saved shortcuts and client colour bands to Xero. It is run by a sole trader based in Lancashire, United Kingdom. This policy explains what the extension and this website do with information.

What the extension can see

The extension only runs on pages at xero.com. It reads the title of the Xero page you are on, so that it can show your recent pages and choose the colour band for the client organisation you are working in. It does not read your accounting data, transactions, contacts or reports.

What the extension stores

  • Your saved shortcuts (a name and a Xero web address) and your client colours (an organisation name and a colour). These are kept in Chrome's own storage and may sync to your other Chrome browsers if you have Chrome sync turned on.
  • Your ten most recent Xero pages (page title and address), kept on your computer only.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Removing the extension deletes it.

Payments

Paid plans are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. When you upgrade, you enter your email address and card details on their payment pages, not in the extension. We receive your email address and whether your subscription is active, so that the extension can unlock Pro features. We never see or store your card number. ExtensionPay's and Stripe's own privacy policies apply to the data they process.

The early access list

If you join the early access list on our website, we receive the email address and any answers you choose to give. We use them only to tell you when early access opens and to decide what to build. We do not sell or share them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

What we never do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use data for advertising or to judge creditworthiness.
  • We do not transfer data to anyone except as described above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email ledgerkeys.help@gmail.com (ledgerkeys.help@gmail.com) and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
